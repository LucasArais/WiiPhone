## Artwork

The six item PNGs are Nintendo promotional artwork, not covered by the software MIT license. Original URLs are preserved in [item-art/SOURCES.md](games/mario-kart/item-art/SOURCES.md). The upstream turf texture and its provenance are preserved in [art/README.md](games/mario-kart/art/README.md). `menu-logo.svg` comes from OpenWii. The optional 3D model pack has been downloaded and converted locally using the upstream pipeline. Source URLs, submitter credits and archive hashes are recorded in [pipeline/sources.json](games/mario-kart/pipeline/sources.json); per-model conversion provenance is stored alongside the GLBs in the ignored `assets/mario-kart/` directory. These Nintendo models are separate from the software MIT license. Original-game fonts and recordings have not been downloaded.
